Questions owners ask

Will Secure365 stop all spam calls, texts and emails?

No service can promise that. We configure available filtering, blocking and reporting controls to reduce unwanted and malicious contact. Carrier and platform limitations apply.

Can you protect every phone and computer?

We will support specified operating systems and device models as integrations are validated. Unsupported devices will be clearly identified rather than represented as protected.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Secure authentication, delegated permissions and customer-approved connections should be used. Never send passwords or MFA codes to HSW365 staff.

Does signing up mean my business is protected immediately?

No. Protection begins only after payment, eligibility checks, installation, configuration and confirmation that each selected security control is active.
