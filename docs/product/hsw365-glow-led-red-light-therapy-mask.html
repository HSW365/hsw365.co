HSW365 GLOW — LED Red Light Therapy Mask

Ships from New Jersey. Shipping confirmed at checkout.

Your skincare routine, upgraded to a device. The HSW365 GLOW is a flexible, full-face LED mask built for a hands-free at-home session in about ten minutes.

How it works

  1. Start with a clean, dry face.
  2. Place the mask over your face and fasten the adjustable strap.
  3. Press the button on the handheld controller to begin a timed session.
  4. Relax. The mask shuts off automatically when the session ends.
  5. Follow with your usual serum or moisturizer.

What you get

  • Flexible matte black silicone mask that contours to the face
  • Red and near-infrared LED array
  • Rechargeable handheld controller with auto shut-off timer
  • Adjustable head strap
  • USB-C charging cable and travel pouch

Good to know

For cosmetic use. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Do not use if you are photosensitive, pregnant, or taking medication that increases light sensitivity without first speaking to your doctor.
