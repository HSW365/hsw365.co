Your neck ages first. Most routines skip it.

SCULPT is a cordless at-home device for the neck, jawline and face. It uses three-color LED light, warm heat and micro-vibration together in one curved head that follows your jaw. Use it for a few minutes a day with your serum or cream.

Three lights, one device

  • Red: targets dullness and the look of fine lines.
  • Blue: for oily, congested skin and visible pores.
  • Green: calms skin that looks red or uneven.

Heat plus vibration

A steady 45°C warm compress paired with high-frequency micro-vibration. It helps your skincare absorb, eases tension in the neck and gives the jawline a firmer feel after each session.

8 modes, multiple intensity levels

Cleanse, infuse, lift and soothe. Aim it at neck lines, smile lines and under the chin.

Built for the curve

The ergonomic head sits flush along the neck and jaw, including the spots your hands miss. One button runs it, so there's nothing to learn.

The routine

  1. Apply your serum or neck cream.
  2. Pick a light and a mode.
  3. Glide upward from collarbone to jaw for a few minutes.

In the box

  • HSW365 SCULPT device
